# 📖 Project Overview — Zesty Platform

Zesty is an enterprise-grade full-stack food discovery and delivery web application built around short video reels. Inspired by modern video platforms like Instagram Reels, Zesty enables local cloud kitchens and chef partners to showcase food preparation videos, driving instant customer orders and hyper-fast delivery.

---

## 🎯 Core Value Propositions

1. **Video-First Discovery**: Customers watch short video reels of actual dishes prepared by local chefs before placing orders.
2. **Transparent 5% Platform Fee**: Transparent pricing structure retaining a low 5% fee for cloud kitchens and local restaurant partners.
3. **Seamless Guest-to-User Experience**: Visitors can browse food reels, add items to a guest cart, and seamlessly merge their cart into an authenticated account upon login.
4. **Multi-Role Portal System**: Decoupled interface supporting Customers, Restaurant Partners, Delivery Riders, and Super Admins.
5. **Real-Time GPS Tracking**: Socket.IO integration streaming live rider location coordinates during active order delivery.
